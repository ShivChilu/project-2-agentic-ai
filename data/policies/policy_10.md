# Policy POL-010: Rare Academic Reference and Archival Acquisitions

- **Document ID:** POL-010
- **Document Title:** Rare Reference Materials and Archival Acquisitions
- **Category:** Reference & Archives
- **Policy Clause:** CLAUSE-REF-10.1
- **Description:** Rare academic reference works, archival treatises, and low-circulation historical compendiums must be deferred pending specific department endowment grants or documented multi-department research utility. Single-investigator niche requests with Low or Medium demand scores must not be purchased from routine funds.
