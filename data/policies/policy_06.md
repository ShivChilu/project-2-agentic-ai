# Policy POL-006: Emerging Technologies and Specialized Research Acquisitions

- **Document ID:** POL-006
- **Document Title:** Advanced Research, Emerging Frontiers, and Quantum Computing
- **Category:** Advanced Research
- **Policy Clause:** CLAUSE-RES-6.1
- **Description:** Specialized research titles in advanced emergent fields such as Quantum Computing, Advanced Photonics, or Theoretical Robotics require documented active course enrollments and verified broad circulation demand (Score >= 70.0). Specialized niche titles exhibiting moderate or speculative interest must be deferred for inter-library loan evaluation.
