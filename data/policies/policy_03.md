# Policy POL-003: Non-Academic and Recreational Collection Exclusions

- **Document ID:** POL-003
- **Document Title:** Non-Academic, Leisure, and Recreational Exclusions
- **Category:** Collection Scope Restrictions
- **Policy Clause:** CLAUSE-EXCL-3.1
- **Description:** Non-academic, recreational, personal hobby, or culinary titles (such as general cooking, personal leisure fiction, hobbyist crafts, or lifestyle guides) are strictly prohibited from acquisition under departmental science and technology acquisition grants. Any request falling outside academic curricular domains must be firmly rejected.
