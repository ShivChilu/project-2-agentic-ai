# Policy POL-012: Robotics and Automation Research Collections

- **Document ID:** POL-012
- **Document Title:** Robotics Research, Mechatronics, and Intelligent Automation
- **Category:** Robotics & Automation
- **Policy Clause:** CLAUSE-ROBO-12.1
- **Description:** Advanced robotics and intelligent automation research texts represent high-value acquisitions. Even when student demand is demonstrated to be high, procurement must be deferred if the current remaining budget is insufficient to absorb the full acquisition cost without causing a negative balance.
