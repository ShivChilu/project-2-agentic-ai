# Policy POL-004: Minimum Demand Circulation Benchmarks

- **Document ID:** POL-004
- **Document Title:** Minimum Historical Demand and Circulation Requirements
- **Category:** Demand Benchmarks
- **Policy Clause:** CLAUSE-DEM-4.1
- **Description:** Book requests submitted for direct purchase approval must demonstrate sustained reader interest with a calculated Demand Score classified as HIGH (score >= 70.0 based on historical checkout circulation and reservations). Books with Low or Medium demand (< 70.0) must be deferred for reconsideration in subsequent acquisition cycles.
