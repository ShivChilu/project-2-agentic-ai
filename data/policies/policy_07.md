# Policy POL-007: High-Cost Acquisitions and Human Committee Review

- **Document ID:** POL-007
- **Document Title:** High-Priced Acquisition Review and Committee Oversight
- **Category:** High-Cost Procurement
- **Policy Clause:** CLAUSE-COST-7.1
- **Description:** Book requests with an individual purchase cost exceeding ₹5,000 must undergo strict financial justification and faculty committee concurrence. If funds are inadequate to cover high-cost acquisitions without depleting essential reserve minimums, the item must be deferred.
