# Policy POL-005: Fiscal Solvency and Budget Ceiling Restrictions

- **Document ID:** POL-005
- **Document Title:** Budget Allocation and Non-Deficit Enforcement
- **Category:** Budget & Finance
- **Policy Clause:** CLAUSE-BUD-5.1
- **Description:** No acquisition proposal shall be approved if the total unit purchase price exceeds the library's currently available remaining budget balance. In no circumstance may the acquisition fund balance enter a negative or deficit state. If the requested price exceeds remaining available funds, the request must be deferred until budget replenishment.
