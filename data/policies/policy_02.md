# Policy POL-002: Duplicate Acquisition Prohibition

- **Document ID:** POL-002
- **Document Title:** Duplicate Acquisition and Existing Inventory Restrictions
- **Category:** Inventory & Duplicates
- **Policy Clause:** CLAUSE-DUP-2.1
- **Description:** A book request that matches an existing title or identical edition currently cataloged and available in the library collection must be rejected as a duplicate. Additional duplicate copies may only be procured if annual historical checkout circulation exceeds 150 instances or waiting reservations exceed 50 students.
