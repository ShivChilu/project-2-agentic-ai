# Policy POL-008: Cloud Computing and Infrastructure Engineering

- **Document ID:** POL-008
- **Document Title:** Cloud Architecture, Distributed Systems, and DevOps Collections
- **Category:** Infrastructure & Cloud
- **Policy Clause:** CLAUSE-CLOUD-8.1
- **Description:** Acquisitions in Cloud Computing, Distributed Infrastructure, Kubernetes, and Microservices are designated high-priority academic assets when supported by strong student demand (Demand Score >= 70.0) and sufficient departmental funding balance. Immediate purchase is authorized when requirements are verified.
