# Policy POL-001: Core Academic Curriculum Alignment

- **Document ID:** POL-001
- **Document Title:** Core Academic Curriculum Alignment and Syllabi Scope
- **Category:** Curriculum & Scope
- **Policy Clause:** CLAUSE-SCOPE-1.1
- **Description:** All book acquisition requests funded from university departmental accounts must directly support accredited academic degree programs (Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, Data Science, and Mathematics). Requests strictly aligned with active syllabus topics receive standard acquisition processing.
